Eight marks

Each one shown three ways, because a mark that only works in one of them is not a mark: large, at the 21px it actually occupies in the navigation bar beside its neighbours, and at 19px in lime, which is the hardest case and the one a favicon has to survive.

A is what the site wears now. It is here to be compared against, not defended.

Drawn as paths in a 96×44 box, one colour, no gradients and no effects, so any of them drops into the existing markup unchanged and inherits currentColor the way the present one does. Nothing here needs a font to be installed or a file to be fetched.
