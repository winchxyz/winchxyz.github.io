Not what it looked like

Twelve times in one session a thing looked like one thing and measured as another. Five of them were mine.

This sheet is not a list of what got built. It is a list of the times the obvious reading was wrong, and what the measurement said instead. It includes my own misses, because the version without them is advertising rather than a record.

The order carries no meaning. These are independent findings, not a sequence, and numbering them would be a claim about the contents that is not true.

Measurement against impression

What looked like something it was not

The material change looked like a separate animation

The orbs turn, and the colour change read as something played on top of a body standing still. The measurement explained the feeling: the anchor of the transition moved 5.9° while the change itself travelled 110° across the body. The geometry of the change was barely coupled to where the orbs were.

The ring cannot carry the front on its own. At 4.9°/s it would need twenty-two seconds to walk the arc, and an exchange lasts two, so both ends have to be read live, every frame.

The pair of forms is chosen by the integer part and weighted by what is left over. That works across the range except at the very top of it. At shape = 3.0 the selector falls through to the frame and the weight is zero, so the frame comes back at full strength.

The instrument lied

Five times the mistake was mine

I wrote "forty-eight orphaned processes" into the README as the cause of the rising compile times. Then I checked them by profile name: not one belonged to the harness. It was the browser of the user.

Limits of the instrument

What the audit could not see

Blind spot
The layout audit compares text against text, line box by line box. The label on the rail is a fixed overlay in a different layer, so its overlap with the text never counted as an intersection. A screenshot from the user found it. That is an honest limit of the tool rather than a failure of it.
Capture
Prefer SHOTS to plain waiting, because scrolling is what wakes the compositor. And force the tier, or you are photographing the fallback.
Accessibility
Headless Chrome reports prefers-reduced-motion: reduce by default, which means every capture in the history of this project was a capture of the calm version of the page rather than the one a visitor sees.
Rule
A measurement that always returns a plausible number is more dangerous than one that throws. A monotonic rise across repeats of the same work is always the instrument, never the work.
